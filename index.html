Privacy Policy – Idea Vault

App: Idea Vault: App Idea Notes (package com.ideavault.app)
Developer: Manas Digital Apps
Effective date: 20 October 2026

Idea Vault is an offline notebook for app ideas, feature requests, bugs and their progress. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: everything you write stays on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or usage information. The app has no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere on its own.

2. Information stored on your device

The ideas, notes, checklists, tags, statuses and screenshots you add are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this information unless you have exported a backup.

3. Photos and screenshots

When you choose to attach a screenshot, the app opens the Android system photo picker. Only the images you select are copied into the app's private storage so they can be shown with your idea. The app does not read the rest of your photo library and does not need any storage or photo permission for this.

4. Backups, import and sharing

You can export a backup file or share an idea as text. These actions only happen when you start them, and the file goes to the location or app you choose (for example your phone's storage, Google Drive or a messaging app). Whatever you share is then handled under the privacy policy of that service. Importing reads only the backup file you pick.

5. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not share data with any third party.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Data security and deletion

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox. You can delete any idea inside the app, delete everything from Backup & Settings → Delete all ideas, or uninstall the app to remove all stored data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
